Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Compact Ventures Sdn Bhd (Company No. 199601033428 / 405780-T) ("Marvelop", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit and use the Marvelop AdSpy platform at https://marvelop.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following circumstances:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you register for automated daily competitor alerts or submit an inquiry, we collect your name, work email address, company name, and the list of competitor URLs you choose to monitor.
  • Public Competitor Queries: Search queries, Facebook page URLs, or brand handles submitted into the search tool to generate intelligence reports.
  • Automated & Technical Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, device information, and anonymous session telemetry to ensure service uptime and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly for legitimate business and product purposes:

  • To generate competitor advertising intelligence reports and dashboards.
  • To scan public Meta Ad Library records and deliver daily launch email notifications when competitors publish new campaigns.
  • To communicate relevant strategy insights, product updates, and customer support responses.
  • To detect and prevent spam, abuse, or unauthorized access to our APIs.

3. Public Data Aggregation & Meta Disclaimer

Marvelop AdSpy indexes and summarizes publicly accessible advertising information provided by the Meta Ad Library in compliance with public disclosure standards. Marvelop does not access, store, or intercept private personal messages, private Facebook profile data, or non-public advertising account financials.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary for operating our platform:

  • Email Delivery: Resend and MailerLite for transactional email alerts and newsletter communication.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting: Railway and Cloudflare for secure server hosting and DDoS mitigation.
  • Authentication: Clerk for user session management and security.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) and access controls to protect your data. Monitored competitor lists and subscriber details are retained until you request account deletion or unsubscribe from alerts.

6. Your Rights & PDPA Compliance

In accordance with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and international standards, you have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can unsubscribe from automated alerts with 1 click using the link in any notification email or by contacting us directly.
